Reading the evidence

Rolling three-year excess return portfolio minus its benchmark, annualised

Each point compares the geometric return of the portfolio with its benchmark over the previous 36 months, before costs. A signal that works would sit mostly above zero. Long stretches on both sides mean the result depends on the period chosen, which is what a 90% interval that includes zero is telling us.
