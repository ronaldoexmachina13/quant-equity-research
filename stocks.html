The 17-stock universe

A fixed, sector-diversified universe of large-cap US stocks, chosen on current size and liquidity rather than past returns. Because the list was drawn from companies that exist today, it carries a mild survivorship bias, discussed in the README's limitations. Every figure below comes from the project's SQLite database: current price and 12-1 momentum from the prices table, Price-to-Book from the book_value table, and each strategy's selection count from re-running the backtest logic month by month.

Reading these cards

Each card shows the stock's latest 12-1 momentum score and Price-to-Book ratio, and how many months each strategy held it: the 48 in-sample months (2021–2024) by default, or the 20 out-of-sample months (January 2025 to August 2026) with the toggle below. A count of 48/48 for Value means the stock ranked among the five cheapest by book value in every month of the backtest, which says more about the persistence of its valuation than a single ratio can. The ratios shown are the latest readings, while selection counts reflect each month's point-in-time ranking, so a stock can look expensive today and still have been a frequent Value holding (WMT is an example).
