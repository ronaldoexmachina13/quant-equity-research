“Removed” means the company left the index at some point in the period (acquired, merged, bankrupt or dropped for size). Free data providers mostly keep no price history for names that no longer trade, so removed companies are far more likely to be missing. The study can only use the names that have data, which is why levels are inflated and only differences are read.
