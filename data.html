A name is usable when it has the price history (and, for Value, the book value) the signal needs. Companies that were acquired or went bankrupt mostly have no free price history, so the gap is largest in the early years. Membership is approximate early on: up to about 12 names may be overstated in 2012.

Coverage by status why survivorship matters

StatusTickersWith price historyWithoutShare without

“Removed” means the company left the index at some point in the sample (acquired, merged, bankrupt or dropped for size). Free providers mostly keep no price history for names that no longer trade, so removed companies are far more likely to be missing. The study can use only names that have data, which is why levels are inflated and differences are also biased, in a direction that depends on the signal.

Names per month and annualised mean return. Descriptive only, not a correction. Names later removed that still had a price history earned a lower average return than names that stayed, Neither the direction nor the size of the effect of the missing names can be measured without delisting data, since acquired companies often earned a premium and bankrupt ones lost most of their value.
