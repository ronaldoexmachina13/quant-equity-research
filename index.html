Do momentum and value still work in the S&P 500?

A pre-specified test of 11-month momentum, price-to-book value and their average on about 500 large US stocks, January 2012 to August 2026.

Finding The data do not show that any of the three signals ranks next-month returns better than chance. The one result that stood out was a portfolio that did worse than its benchmark. The design was committed before the results existed and every test is counted. The test could only have detected a large effect, so this is an absence of evidence and not a proof of no effect.

Relative performance top-decile portfolio against the equal-weight benchmark of the same stocks

Cumulative return of each top-decile portfolio divided by that of its own equal-weight benchmark, before costs, on a log scale. A line above 1.0 means the portfolio is ahead. Momentum needs 11 months of history, so it has its own benchmark; Value and Combined share one. Prices come from stocks that were in the index at some point and are still tradable today, which flatters every line; the Universe page measures how much. The Portfolios page has growth, drawdowns and costs.

Specification what was tested
Universe
S&P 500 members, about 500 stocks, 176 months from January 2012 to August 2026
Signals
Momentum: 11-month return, no skipped month. Value: negative price-to-book. Combined: average of the two z-scores
Primary tests
Mean monthly rank IC, quintile spread, Fama-MacBeth slope, each with a 90% bootstrap interval
Portfolios
Top decile and top quintile, equal weight, monthly rebalance, against an equal-weight benchmark. Costs of 10 bps one way are shown separately
Periods
2012 to 2020 (108 months) is the new test. 2021 to 2026 (68 months) is a re-test, because those months were seen in an earlier 17-stock study
Timing
Signals use data to the end of the previous month. Book values enter on the filing date
Limits what this page does not show
  • It does not show that the signals are worthless. The test has power only for a rank IC above about 0.055.
  • It does not show out-of-sample performance. No period was held back, and the 2021 to 2026 months were already known.
  • It does not remove survivorship bias. Removed index members are under-represented in the price data. See the Universe page.
  • It does not test other signals, horizons or rebalance frequencies. The ledger above counts the registered tests and the interval variants. The sensitivity, alpha and four-factor results are reported on the Signals and Portfolios pages and in the amendments, not in this ledger.
