Does buying recent winners actually beat just holding everything?

A from-scratch quant research project testing three systematic equity strategies — momentum, value, and a blend of both — against a 17-stock universe over 2021–2024, backtested honestly and reported the way the data actually came out.

The headline finding: averaged over the full 4-year window, a passive benchmark that just holds all 17 stocks equally beat every active strategy tested, on a risk-adjusted basis. But split that same window into 2021–2022 and 2023–2024, and two different strategies each independently beat the benchmark in their own regime — the full-period average simply washed both wins out.
