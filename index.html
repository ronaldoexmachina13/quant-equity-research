Can simple factor strategies beat holding the whole universe?

A quantitative research project testing three systematic equity strategies (momentum, value, and a blend of the two) against a passive equal-weight benchmark on a 17-stock universe over 2021–2024. Results are reported as the backtest produced them, including where the active strategies fell short.

Headline finding: over the full four-year window, a passive benchmark holding all 17 stocks in equal weight delivered a higher Sharpe ratio than every active strategy tested. Splitting the window changes the picture: Value led the benchmark in 2021–2022 and Combined led in 2023–2024, a pattern the full-period average conceals. Neither lead is statistically significant on 24 months of data, so the regime pattern is a hypothesis worth testing further rather than an established edge.
