Can simple factor strategies beat holding the whole universe?

A quantitative research project testing three systematic equity strategies (momentum, value, and a blend of the two) against a passive equal-weight benchmark on a 17-stock universe over 2021–2024. Results are reported as the backtest produced them, including where the active strategies fell short.

Headline finding: no active strategy could be distinguished from a passive benchmark holding all 17 stocks in equal weight. Over the full four-year window Value had a slightly higher Sharpe ratio than the benchmark (0.86 vs 0.81), but it carried more market exposure (beta 1.06) and its lead depends on a handful of names. Splitting the window, Value edged the benchmark in 2021–2022 and Combined led in 2023–2024. None of these differences is statistically significant on 24 to 48 months of data, so they were treated as hypotheses for a pre-registered out-of-sample test. On the 20 months that followed (January 2025 to August 2026), the benchmark had the highest Sharpe ratio of the four; Value's alpha faded to almost zero, while Combined's alpha kept its direction, though not at a significant level.
