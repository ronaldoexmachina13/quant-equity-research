Momentum and value in S&P 500 stocks

Pre-registered test of 12-1 momentum, price-to-book value and their average on about 500 S&P 500 members, January 2012 to August 2026. Signals are judged on rank IC, quintile spread and Fama-MacBeth slopes. Top-decile and top-quintile equal-weight portfolios are secondary evidence. Every interval is a 90% bootstrap interval.

Bottom line No signal shows a detectable cross-sectional relationship with next-month returns in the primary tests: none of the six rank IC intervals excludes zero. Two of 18 portfolio Sharpe-difference intervals do, both Value in 2012 to 2020 and both the same overlapping bet; about 1.8 would be expected under the null at the 90% level. With 108 months and a monthly IC standard deviation of up to about 0.23, the minimum detectable mean IC is about 0.055, so the evidence is consistent with no effect and with a small one. The data are free and survivor-biased: relative comparisons are informative, return levels are not.
Scorecard estimate and 90% bootstrap interval, before costs

Rank IC, quintile spread and Fama-MacBeth slope are the primary tests of whether a signal ranks stocks by next-month return. The Sharpe difference is the top-decile portfolio against its equal-weight benchmark and is secondary evidence. Red marks an interval that excludes zero. The 2021 to 2026 months were known from the 17-stock study, so they are a re-test and not out-of-sample evidence.
